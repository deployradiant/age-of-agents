# Age of Agents — Color & Mood Palettes
## Studio Ghibli × Impressionism × Age of Empires (Antiquity)

All colors are expressed as hex + CSS-compatible. The goal: warm memory,
hand-painted feel, soft light, earthen tones with jewel-like accents.

## 🎨 IMPRESSIONIST TECHNIQUES (Style Notes)

For the pixel-art / painted approach:
- **No pure black** — shadows = sienna + ultramarine mixes
- **No pure white** — highlights = warm cream or sky reflection
- **Broken color** — tile textures should have visible brushstrokes /
  color patches (à la Monet haystacks)
- **Atmospheric perspective** — far objects shift toward `#b4c4d4`
  (hazy blue-grey)
- **Warm light source** — everything slightly golden on one side,
  cool blue-violet on the shadow side
